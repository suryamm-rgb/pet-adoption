# Frontend: Screens by role

[← Frontend index](README.md)

| Role | Screens |
|---|---|
| Everyone | Account settings (`/account`): edit details, verify phone (6 digit code), notification switches (email, browser, WhatsApp), change password, sign out of all devices, delete account. Login and sign-up offer Continue with Google when the site has a Google client id |
| Public | Home, Find caretakers (filters and sorting in the URL), Caretaker profile with booking form and live price, Adopt, Pets for sale, Listing detail (adopt request, buy, chat), Shop, Cart and checkout, Login, Register, Forgot and reset password, Verify email, Legal pages |
| Owner | Dashboard, My pets, Add and edit pet (health, care, behaviour, emergency, vaccination upload, change history), My bookings, Booking detail (timeline, activity feed, daily updates, locations map, instructions, chat, pay, cancel with refund preview, complete, dispute, review, invoice), Payments, Reviews (reply to a review, report it), Adoption requests, Shop orders (cancel with refund) |
| Caretaker | Dashboard, Profile and verification (documents, submit), Booking requests (accept or decline), Stays and stay detail (post update, acknowledge instructions, emergency), Availability calendar (click days to block them), Bank account, Automatic payouts setup (when the site runs Route), Earnings, Reviews (reply, report). Stay page: post updates, **Share location** |
| Seller | My listings, New listing, Incoming adoption requests with chat, Products, Shop orders (pack, ship, deliver, cancel) |
| Admin | Dashboard, Verification queue, Listing approvals, All bookings and detail (read-only chat), Payments and payouts (release, refund), Disputes (decide), Users (block), Reports (moderation queue), Audit log, Settings (fee, refund rules, deadlines), Shop orders. Long lists are paged 25 at a time |
